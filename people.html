Hyeongil Nam 남현길
Principal Investigator · Hanyang University ERICA
ICT Convergence (Undergraduate) / Human–Computer Interaction (Graduate) 한양대학교 ERICA · ICT융합학부(학부) / HCI(대학원)

Hyeongil Nam works at the intersection of computer vision and graphics to build the foundational technologies and applications behind AR/VR systems. His research spans multimodal interaction — from embodied agents to diverse forms of XR interaction — applied to healthcare, industry and beyond, alongside foundational work in real-world 3D reconstruction, Physical AI and digital twins.
