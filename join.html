Join us 함께 하기

Come in and ask before you apply. 지원하기 전에, 먼저 들어와서 물어보세요.

We're looking for graduate and undergraduate researchers interested in spatial computing and 3D representation, embodied and spatial interaction, human understanding and embodied agents, or Physical AI and digital twins. You do not need publications — a clear interest in one of the four areas and the willingness to build things matter more.

공간 컴퓨팅과 3D 복원, 체화된·공간적 상호작용, 인간 이해와 체화된 에이전트, Physical AI와 디지털 트윈에 관심 있는 대학원생과 학부 연구생을 상시 모집합니다. 논문 실적은 필요하지 않습니다.

Who can apply 지원 자격

Graduate applicants (MS / PhD) through Hanyang ERICA, undergraduate research interns from any department, and visiting or exchange researchers.

What to prepare 준비물

A short CV, a transcript, and a few sentences about what you would like to work on. A course project, a demo or a repository says more than a grade.

When 일정

Graduate admission runs twice a year; the exact windows change, so check the graduate school's announcements — and talk to the lab before you apply. Interns are taken all year round.

The virtual lab has an admissions corner with the same three boards, and an advising desk where your question reaches us directly.

가상 연구실의 입학 상담 코너에 같은 내용이 있고, 상담 데스크에 질문을 남기면 그대로 전달됩니다.

  • Spatial Computing
  • Vision, Graphics & 3D Representation
  • Embodied & Spatial Interaction
  • XR, Multimodal & Body-Based Interaction
  • Human Understanding & Embodied Agents
  • Human Behavior & Adaptive Interaction
  • Physical AI & Digital Twins
  • Real-World Intelligent Systems
