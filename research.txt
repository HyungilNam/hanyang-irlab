badge: 01 — Understand & Represent
icon: represent
title: Spatial Computing / Vision, Graphics & 3D Representation
ko: 사람과 공간을 기계가 이해할 수 있는 형태로 복원하고, 다시 사실적으로 그려냅니다.
body: Reconstructing the geometry, material and layout of real objects and spaces, and rendering them convincingly — the representation layer everything else stands on. REPAINT, real-object XR creation, grip-lift interaction.

---

badge: 02 — Interact
icon: interact
title: Embodied & Spatial Interaction / XR, Multimodal & Body-Based Interaction
ko: 화면과 마우스가 아니라 몸과 공간을 입력으로 삼는 상호작용을 설계합니다.
body: Interaction that takes the body and the space around it as its input rather than a screen and a mouse: avatar locomotion, haptic feedback and multi-user scenarios — AR Fitness Dog, avatar–locomotion congruence, asynchronous avatars, Watch Buddy.

---

badge: 03 — Understand & Adapt
icon: adapt
title: Human Understanding & Embodied Agents / Human Behavior, Social & Adaptive Interaction
ko: 음성·제스처·시선에서 사람의 상태를 읽어 내고, 그에 맞춰 반응하는 에이전트를 만듭니다.
body: Reading a person's cognitive, emotional and social state from voice, gesture and gaze — and writing it as behavior an embodied agent can actually run, as in the emotionally responsive virtual parent.

---

badge: 04 — Connect to Reality
icon: connect
title: Physical AI & Digital Twins / Real-World Intelligent Systems
ko: 물리 세계에서 실제로 작동하는 AI와, 현장에 적용되는 디지털 트윈을 만듭니다.
body: Intelligence that acts in the physical world, and twins deployed where the stakes are real: hydrogen and pipeline safety (SHAPE), nursing simulation, manufacturing.
